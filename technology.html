Technology & validation

Complete antenna measurement, without a far-field range

The TTC3000 measures each antenna port in the near field and computes the far-field pattern in software. A large multi-port antenna can be fully characterised in a compact, movable chamber.

System architecture

Four subsystems under one control console

01

Movable RF anechoic chamber

An integrated, shielded chamber, 100 MHz to 8 GHz, that needs no dedicated room.

02

Cylindrical near-field scanner

A probe scanner moves along the antenna axis while the AUT rotator turns the antenna, sampling a cylinder around it.

03

Dual-path RF switch matrix

Switches every antenna port to the instruments automatically, so ports are never changed by hand.

04

Instruments and software

A vector network analyzer, optional PIM analyzers and RET control, run by test scripts that load from the barcode.

Near-field to far-field

Patterns computed, not assumed

Near-field data from the cylindrical scan are transformed into far-field radiation patterns. Agreement with theoretical near-field to far-field data is 97% or better.

Because the scan surrounds the antenna, the system does not need to be aligned to the antenna's phase centre, and all ports are measured in one set-up through the switch matrix.

The software displays patterns in polar and Cartesian coordinates, overlays multiple patterns for comparison, and exports reports to Word and Excel with more than 20 pattern parameters per port and frequency.

Independent validation

CTTL measurement audit

China Telecommunication Technology Labs (CTTL), part of the China Academy of Information and Communications Technology (CAICT), conducted a measurement audit of the gain measurement capability of Voyager's Vehicle-Mounted Testing Platform.

  • Method. Stable low-band and high-band base station antennas were measured, and the results were compared with the robust average of data from China's major testing laboratories.
  • Result. The gain measured by Voyager's platform was consistent with the results of the mainstream laboratories. The audit report was issued on 8 July 2026.
  • Technical basis. GB/T 9410 general specification for mobile communication antennas; YD/T 2867 and YD/T 2868 on passive base station antenna requirements and measurement methods.
  • Cooperation. Voyager and CTTL signed a strategic cooperation agreement in November 2025.

CTTL was established in 1981 and provides testing, certification, metrology and consulting services to government bodies, operators and manufacturers in the ICT sector.

Industry recognition

Reviewed on site by China Institute of Communications experts

The China Institute of Communications (CIC), China's national professional society for the communications industry, has twice sent expert teams to our antenna testing division in Dongguan to examine our rapid base station antenna test equipment.

November 2025

On 22–23 November 2025 a CIC expert team studied our rapid antenna test equipment on site, reviewed its design principles, operating procedure and test performance, and proposed design improvements.

March 2026

Second expert visit

On 7 March 2026 a second expert team, with specialists from research institutes, antenna and equipment manufacturers, universities and an antenna industry alliance, assessed the improvements made since the first visit and reviewed a draft group standard on technical requirements for rapid fault-detection equipment for passive base station antennas.
