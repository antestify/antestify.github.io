Base station antenna test equipment and telecom services

Voyager is a telecom test and service group founded in Xi'an, China, in 1997. We design and build base station antenna test equipment, and provide third-party antenna testing, antenna refurbishment and telecom equipment repair and maintenance for operators, tower companies and antenna manufacturers.

Our business

Test equipment, third-party antenna testing, antenna refurbishment, and telecom equipment repair and maintenance, covering antennas at the factory, on delivery, before installation and after repair.

01

Test equipment

Products

Voyager designs, builds and sells ANTestify base station antenna test equipment: the TTC3000 Integrated Testing System for production lines, warehouses and repair centres, and the Vehicle-Mounted Testing Platform for work in the field. Radiation, circuit, PIM and RET tests in one station.

 02

Third-party antenna testing

Testing services

Independent testing of base station antennas for operators, tower companies and antenna manufacturers: arrival inspection, pre-installation testing, and spare-parts and on-site testing for emergency readiness.

Our history

Voyager was founded in Xi'an in 1997 on in-house test systems and repair services for operators and equipment vendors. In 2021 it set up its antenna testing division in Songshan Lake, Dongguan, with senior professors from the Institute of Antennas and Electromagnetic Scattering at Xidian University. The division develops ANTestify antenna test equipment and leads the group's international business.
