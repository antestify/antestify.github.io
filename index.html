Why it matters

Antenna testing has not kept pace with the network

Antenna production lines now finish an antenna every few minutes, and a mature network holds hundreds of thousands of sites. Yet full radiation testing still usually means a far-field range or a multi-probe spherical near-field system: a dedicated building, high investment and days of measurement for one large multi-port antenna. In the field, a VSWR check alone cannot find a radiation fault.

The TTC3000 brings the complete measurement into a compact, movable station.
