About Voyager

From network maintenance to antenna testing

Voyager has kept telecom networks running since 1997. Every repair line we ran was backed by test systems we designed ourselves. The TTC3000 antenna test family comes from that same engineering approach.

Who we are

One group, one engineering heritage

Voyager is a telecom test and service group founded in Xi'an, China, in 1997, and one of the earliest telecom equipment repair companies in China. We built our business on in-house test systems and repair services for operators and equipment makers, and are recognised as a National High-Tech Enterprise.

In 2021 we set up our antenna testing division in Songshan Lake, Dongguan, together with senior professors from the Institute of Antennas and Electromagnetic Scattering at Xidian University. It brings our nearly 30 years of test-system development together with deep antenna measurement expertise, develops the ANTestify antenna test systems and leads our international business.

Our journey

Each stage builds on the one before

  1. 1997

    Voyager is founded in Xi'an, one of the earliest telecom equipment repair companies in China.

  2. 2000

    The founder's entrepreneurial story is featured by China Central Television (CCTV).

  3. 2000s

    Six series of in-house test systems: switching, PHS base stations, telecom power, digital microwave, broadband access and GSM/CDMA base stations.

  4. 2010s

    GSM base station simulation and telecom power test platforms appraised by the O&M Committee of the China Association of Communication Enterprises.

  5. 2012–15

    Algeria. A Voyager service team based in Algiers provides telecom equipment repair services as an independent repair company.

  6. 2021

    Antenna testing division set up in Dongguan with antenna experts from Xidian University.

  7. 2024

    Antenna testing, repair and refurbishment programme starts for China Mobile Hainan.

  8. 2025

    Strategic cooperation agreement with CTTL. Repair contract for repeaters and antennas with China Telecom Xi'an (2025–2027). First expert visit organised by the China Institute of Communications to assess our rapid antenna test equipment.

  9. 2026

    Second expert visit by the China Institute of Communications, including review of a draft group standard for rapid fault-detection equipment for passive base station antennas. CTTL measurement audit of gain measurement capability. International market launch.

International experience

On the ground in Algiers, 2012–2015

From 2012 to 2015 Voyager stationed a service team in Algiers, the capital of Algeria, to repair telecom network equipment in service. Voyager worked as an independent repair company, with its own engineers, test platforms and repair processes, alongside the project teams of Chinese equipment vendors, including Huawei.

Running a repair operation about 9,000 km from home shaped how we support customers and partners abroad today, with our own tools and our own processes.

What we stand for

Make complete antenna testing fast and affordable enough for every antenna

At the factory, at the warehouse, before installation and after repair. We want to be the testing partner that antenna manufacturers and network operators rely on, so networks perform better at lower cost and with less waste.
